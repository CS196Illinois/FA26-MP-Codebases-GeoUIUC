## Requirements recorded as choices (not looked up)

- Systems track: **CS 233 & CS 341**, or **CS 340 + two 400-level CS courses** (excluding the courses the catalog names)
- Statistics: one of STAT 200, STAT 212, CS 361 (CS 361 recommended)
- Two additional GIS courses from the catalog list
- Two human and/or physical geography courses from the catalog list

## Required courses excluded by the rules

- Mathematics (MATH 220/221, MATH 225/257, MATH 231)
- LAS 100/101/102 (orientation)
- CS 100 (recommended)
