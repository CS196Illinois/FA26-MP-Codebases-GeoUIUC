## Buildings

Buildings counted for this major, with the core courses that have lectures there:

- **Campus Instructional Facility**: CS 173, CS 222, CS 374, CS 421
- **Foellinger Auditorium**: CS 225
- **Music Building**: MUS 101, MUS 102, MUS 105, MUS 107, MUS 108, MUS 110, MUS 202, MUS 205, MUS 207, MUS 209, MUS 305, MUS 313, MUS 314
- **Noyes Laboratory**: CS 173
- **Siebel Center for Comp Sci**: CS 173, CS 448
- **Smith Memorial Hall**: MUS 201, MUS 202, MUS 207, MUS 208

## Requirements recorded as choices (not looked up)

- CS 233 & CS 341, or CS 340 + two 400-level CS courses
- CS 361, or CS 427 as a substitute for students interested in systems building
- Senior Project or Senior Thesis (catalog lists MUS 299)
- Keyboard proficiency: MUS 172 and/or MUS 173 unless competency is shown another way

## Required courses excluded by the rules

- Math (MATH 220/221, MATH 231, MATH 225/257)
- FAA 101, MUS 100 (orientation)

## Gaps and notes

- Not offered in Fall 2026, so **Spring 2026** data is used: MUS 102, MUS 202, MUS 108, MUS 208, MUS 110, MUS 314, MUS 105, MUS 209, MUS 305, CS 448
- Offered, but only online or non-lecture sections (no building): CS 124, CS 128, MUS 407
- **CS 448** (Audio Computing Laboratory) is required in the catalog's CS section. It is not offered in Fall 2026, so its Spring 2026 data is used (see table).
- **ECE 402** (Electronic Music Synthesis) is required under the catalog's *Engineering* heading. It is neither a CS nor a MUS course, so under rule 2 it is **not counted**. Flagged for review.
- MUS 172/173 (keyboard) are conditional, and the senior project wording is unclear, so they are recorded as choices, not core.
