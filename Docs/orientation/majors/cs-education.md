## Requirements recorded as choices (not looked up)

- Systems track: **CS 233 & CS 341**, or **CS 340 + two 400-level CS courses** (excluding the courses the catalog names)
- CS 357 or CS 421
- Learning Sciences: EPOL 201 or EPOL 202
- Learning Sciences: 15 hours of College of Education electives

## Required courses excluded by the rules

- Mathematical Foundations: MATH 220/221, MATH 231, one of MATH 225/227/257
- EDUC 101 (orientation)

## Gaps and notes

- Not offered in Fall 2026, so **Spring 2026** data is used: CI 211, CI 489, CI 401, CI 404, CI 473
- **Not offered in Fall 2026 or Spring 2026** (no building recorded): CI 382
- Offered, but only online or non-lecture sections (no building): CS 124, CS 128, CI 404, EDPR 442, EDUC 202
- Unclear building names, **not counted**: "Library" (EPSY 201); "Location Pending" (CI 403)
- The catalog treats this as **one major with two concentrations**. Both are documented here; the shared core applies to both.
- CS 361 is listed under *Mathematical Foundations*, but it is a required CS course, so it is counted.
