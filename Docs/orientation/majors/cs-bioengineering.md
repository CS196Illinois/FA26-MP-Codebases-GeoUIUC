## Buildings

Buildings counted for this major, with the core courses that have lectures there:

- **Campus Instructional Facility**: BIOE 205, BIOE 310, CS 173, CS 222, CS 374
- **Foellinger Auditorium**: CS 225
- **Noyes Laboratory**: CS 173
- **Siebel Center for Comp Sci**: CS 173

## Requirements recorded as choices (not looked up)

- Systems track: **CS 233 & CS 341**, or **CS 340 + two 400-level CS courses** (excluding the courses the catalog names)
- CS 357 or CS 421
- One CS technical elective (400-level above CS 403)
- BIOE 206 or BIOE 302
- MATH 257 or BIOE 210
- CHEM 102 & CHEM 103, or MCB 150
- Bioengineering technical electives: 15 hours

## Required courses excluded by the rules

- Foundational math and science (MATH 221, MATH 231, MATH 241, MATH 285, PHYS 211, PHYS 212)
- Orientation: ENG 100, BIOE 100, BIOE 120
- CS 100 (optional)

## Gaps and notes

- Not offered in Fall 2026, so **Spring 2026** data is used: BIOE 205
- **Not offered in Fall 2026 or Spring 2026** (no building recorded): BIOE 404
- Offered, but only online or non-lecture sections (no building): CS 124, CS 128
- BIOE 310 is listed under *Foundational Mathematics and Science*, but it is a required BIOE course, so it is counted.
