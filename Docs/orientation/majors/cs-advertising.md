## Buildings

- **Armory**: ADV 284
- **Business Instructional Fac**: ADV 250
- **Campus Instructional Facility**: CS 173, CS 222, CS 374, CS 421
- **Foellinger Auditorium**: CS 225
- **Gregory Hall**: ADV 390, ADV 461, ADV 492
- **Materials Science & Eng Bld**: CS 361
- **Natural History Building**: CS 340
- **Noyes Laboratory**: CS 173
- **Roger Adams Laboratory**: ADV 281
- **Siebel Center for Comp Sci**: CS 173

## Requirements recorded as choices (not looked up)

- ADV 360 or ADV 483
- Two 400-level CS technical electives (above CS 403, not CS 491)
- Advertising electives: any ADV courses not already required (6 hours)

## Required courses excluded by the rules

- Mathematical Foundations (MATH 220/221, MATH 231, MATH 225/257)
- College of Media Foundations: one of ANTH 103 / PSYC 100 / SOC 100; ECON 102; ECON 103 or ECON 302; BADM 320 (required, but not CS or ADV courses)

## Gaps and notes

- Offered, but only online or non-lecture sections (no building): CS 124, CS 128
- CS 361 is listed under the catalog's *Mathematical Foundations* heading, but it is a required CS course, so it is counted (see README, rule 2).
