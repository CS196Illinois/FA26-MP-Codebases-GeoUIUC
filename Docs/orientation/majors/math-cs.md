## Requirements recorded as choices (not looked up)

- Systems track: **CS 233 & CS 341**, or **CS 340 + two 400-level CS courses** (excluding the courses the catalog names)
- MATH 257, MATH 415 or MATH 416
- Six advanced MATH/CS courses: one from each of Groups I-V plus one more 400-level course

## Required courses excluded by the rules

- Calculus (MATH 220/221, MATH 231, MATH 241)
- LAS 100/101/102 (orientation)
- CS 100 (optional)
