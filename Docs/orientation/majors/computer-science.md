## Requirements recorded as choices (not looked up)

- Orientation: CS 210 or CS 211
- Technical electives: six courses (18 hours) from CS 397 and CS 400-level, including one team project course and three courses from one focus area

## Required courses excluded by the rules

- Foundational Mathematics and Science (MATH 221/220, MATH 231, MATH 241, MATH 257/415/416, PHYS 211, PHYS 212, science elective)
- ENG 100 (orientation)
- CS 100 (recommended, optional)
