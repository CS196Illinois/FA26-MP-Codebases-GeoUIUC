- **Siebel Center for Comp Sci**: CS 173

## Requirements recorded as choices (not looked up)

- CS 357 or CS 421
- Systems track: **CS 233 & CS 341**, or **CS 340 + two 400-level CS courses** (excluding the courses the catalog names)
- Applied Animal Sciences courses: choose 3
- Basic Animal Sciences courses: choose 3
- Communication option: CMN 101, ALEC 115, or CMN 111 & CMN 112
- ECON 102 or ACE 100

## Required courses excluded by the rules

- Department Foundation math and science: CHEM 102 & 103, CHEM 104 & 105, MATH 220/221, MATH 225/257, MATH 231
- CS 100 (orientation)

- Not offered in Fall 2026, so **Spring 2026** data is used: ANSC 223, ANSC 224
- Offered, but only online or non-lecture sections (no building): CS 124, CS 128, ANSC 398
- Unclear building names, **not counted**: "Library" (ANSC 224)
- CS 361 is listed under *Department Foundation*, but it is a required CS course, so it is counted.
