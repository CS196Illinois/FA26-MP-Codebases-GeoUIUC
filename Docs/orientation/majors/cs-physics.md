## Buildings

Buildings counted for this major, with the core courses that have lectures there:

- **Campus Instructional Facility**: CS 173, CS 222, CS 374
- **Chemistry Annex**: PHYS 435
- **Foellinger Auditorium**: CS 225
- **Loomis Laboratory**: PHYS 213, PHYS 214, PHYS 225, PHYS 325
- **Noyes Laboratory**: CS 173
- **Siebel Center for Comp Sci**: CS 173, PHYS 446

## Requirements recorded as choices (not looked up)

- Systems track: **CS 233 & CS 341**, or **CS 340 + two 400-level CS courses** (excluding the courses the catalog names)
- CS 357 or CS 450
- One CS technical elective (400-level above CS 403)
- CS 361 or STAT 400
- PHYS 486 or PHYS 485
- PHYS technical electives: 14 hours of CS or PHYS 300/400-level

## Required courses excluded by the rules

- Calculus and linear algebra (MATH 221, MATH 231, MATH 241, MATH 257/416, MATH 285)
- PHYS 211, PHYS 212 (introductory university physics)
- ENG 100, PHYS 110 (orientation)
- CS 100 (optional)

## Gaps and notes

- Offered, but only online or non-lecture sections (no building): CS 124, CS 128, PHYS 246
- PHYS 213 and PHYS 214 are listed under *Foundational Mathematics and Science*. They are counted because they are Physics courses required of this major, while PHYS 211/212 are excluded as the general introductory physics sequence (see README, rule 3). Flagged for review.
