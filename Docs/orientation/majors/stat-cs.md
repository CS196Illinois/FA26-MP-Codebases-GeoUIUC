## Requirements recorded as choices (not looked up)

- Systems track: **CS 233 & CS 341**, or **CS 340 + two 400-level CS courses** (excluding the courses the catalog names)
- One of STAT 107, STAT 200, STAT 212
- One statistical application elective (STAT 428, 431, 432, 434, 448)
- One computational application elective (CS 410, 411, 412, 446, 481, 482)

- Mathematical Foundation (calculus through MATH 241; MATH 257 or MATH 415)
- CS 100 (recommended)
