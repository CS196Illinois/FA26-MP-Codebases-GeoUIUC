## Buildings

Buildings counted for this major, with the core courses that have lectures there:

- **Campus Instructional Facility**: CS 173, CS 222, CS 374, CS 421
- **Foellinger Auditorium**: CS 225
- **Materials Science & Eng Bld**: CS 361
- **National Soybean Res Ctr**: CPSC 440
- **Noyes Laboratory**: CS 173
- **Siebel Center for Comp Sci**: CS 173
- **Turner Hall**: CPSC 102, CPSC 112

## Requirements recorded as choices (not looked up)

- CPSC 393 or CPSC 395
- Systems track: **CS 233 & CS 341**, or **CS 340 + two 400-level CS courses** (excluding the courses the catalog names)
- Communication option: CMN 101, ALEC 115, or CMN 111 & CMN 112
- Crop Sciences electives (one 400-level CPSC/HORT/PLPA course, and one more CPSC/HORT/PLPA course)

## Required courses excluded by the rules

- Mathematical Foundations (MATH 220/221, MATH 225/257/415/416, MATH 231)
- ACES 101 (Department Foundation; not a CS or CPSC course)
- CS 100 (recommended)

## Gaps and notes

- **Not offered in Fall 2026 or Spring 2026** (no building recorded): CPSC 212
- Offered, but only online or non-lecture sections (no building): CS 124, CS 128, CPSC 498
- Unclear building names, **not counted**: "Location Pending" (CPSC 444)
- CS 361 is listed under *Mathematical Foundations*, but it is a required CS course, so it is counted.
- The catalog lists CPSC 212 without a title.
