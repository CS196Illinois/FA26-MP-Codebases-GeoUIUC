# Freshman Mode: Selection Criteria and Building List

Freshman Mode draws rounds only from places that almost every new undergraduate at
UIUC uses or visits in their first semester, regardless of major. This file explains
exactly which places are included, which are left out and why, and where every entry
comes from.

- **Research date:** October 18, 2026. All pages below were read on this date.
- **Status:** list approved. Coordinates and Street View coverage are not chosen yet;
  that happens in a later step and will be documented separately.

---

## 1. Core rule

A place is included only if **both** of these are true:

1. **Everyday relevance:** it belongs to one of the five categories in section 2, which
   cover where first-year students live, eat, study, get services, and orient
   themselves on campus. These do not depend on a student's major.
2. **Official source:** it is listed on the website of the UIUC unit responsible for it
   (University Housing, the University Library, Campus Recreation, and so on). No entry is
   based on general knowledge, unofficial guides, or community wikis.

Buildings used mainly for **classes** are deliberately left to **Major Mode**, which
picks buildings from real Fall 2026 course data. There is no official source that says
which classroom buildings "every freshman" uses, so we do not guess.

## 2. Included categories

| # | Category | Why it is included | Official source |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Undergraduate residence halls | Most first-year students live in University Housing. | [University Housing: Undergraduate Halls][housing-halls] |
| 2 | All-you-care-to-eat dining halls | Where students living in the halls eat every day. Retail cafés are not included (see section 4). | [University Housing: Dining Locations][housing-dining] |
| 3 | General study libraries | Large libraries that serve all students, not one subject. | [University Library: Library Maps][library-maps] |
| 4 | Student life and services | The student union, the recreation centers, and the campus health center. | Each unit's own website (see table) |
| 5 | Campus landmarks | Landmarks new students are shown during orientation and campus visits. | [Undergraduate Admissions: Self-Guided Quad Tour][quad-tour] |

### How residence halls are grouped

Residence halls follow the **groupings on the University Housing page exactly**. When the
page lists several buildings as one complex (for example, "Pennsylvania Avenue Residence
Halls (PAR): Babcock, Blaisdell, Carr and Saunders"), that complex is **one** location in
the game. The buildings in a complex stand next to each other and cannot be told apart
reliably from Street View.

### How dining halls inside residence halls are handled

Three of the four dining halls are located **inside** a residence hall complex
(ISR, LAR and PAR). Each of these is **merged** with its residence hall into a single
location, so the same spot never appears twice. The Ikenberry Dining Center is in its own
building (the Student Dining and Residential Programs Building, SDRP), so it is a
separate location.

### How campus landmarks are chosen

The landmark list comes from the 22 stops on the official
[Self-Guided Quad Tour][quad-tour] run by Undergraduate Admissions. A stop is included as a
landmark if it is a monument, statue, open space, historic site or public venue. A stop is
**not** included if:

- it is mainly a departmental classroom or office building, because classroom buildings
  belong to Major Mode;
- it is already in another category (for example, Main Library, Illini Union, Illinois
  Street Residence Halls); or
- it is an area rather than a single place (Campustown / Green Street).

Section 4 lists every tour stop that was excluded, with the reason.

## 4. Considered but not included

### Categories left out

| Excluded | Reason |
|---|---|
| Classroom and department buildings | Covered by Major Mode, which uses real Fall 2026 course data. No official source says which classroom buildings every freshman uses. |
| Subject (branch) libraries, e.g. Chemistry Library (Noyes Laboratory), Music and Performing Arts Library, Veterinary Medicine Library, Ricker Library of Architecture and Art | They serve specific majors, not all students. |
| Athletics venues (Memorial Stadium, State Farm Center) | Landmarks, but not places students use day to day. They are also not stops on the Self-Guided Quad Tour. |
| Retail dining and cafés (57 North, The Caffeinator, Corner Café, InfiniTEA, TerraByte, Urbana South Market) | All are inside buildings already on the list (SDRP, FAR, ISR, PAR), so they add no new location. |
| Graduate and private housing | Not where first-year undergraduates usually live. |
| Other Campus Recreation listings (Campus Bike Center, Ice Arena, Outdoor, Facility Rentals) | Not used by most new students; "Outdoor" and "Facility Rentals" are programs, not buildings. |

## 5. Known limitations

- **Undergraduate Library status:** sources confirm the 2022 closure and the renovation
  plan, but we did not find an official page confirming the renovation is finished or
  what the building is called now. It is excluded either way.
- **Missing addresses:** several source pages do not show street addresses. Coordinates
  will be chosen in a later step, and how each one is chosen will be documented.
- **Grouped residence halls:** a complex such as PAR is one location, so the game does not
  teach the individual building names inside it.
- **Judgement calls:** whether a Quad Tour stop is a "landmark" or a "classroom building"
  (e.g. Foellinger Auditorium, Altgeld Hall) follows the rule in section 2. A different
  reasonable rule could give a slightly different list.
- **Lists change:** halls get renamed or rebuilt and tours change. The list reflects the
  official pages on October 18, 2026.
